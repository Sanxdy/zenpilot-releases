What ZenPilot is not

ZenPilot is not an anti-detect browser. It does not defeat platform abuse or bot detection, and it makes no promise about whether an account gets flagged, restricted, or banned. It runs stock Chromium — the engine is not modified.

"Stealth mode" keeps a profile's reported values consistent across sign-ins. That is a consistency feature, not a disguise, and it is not sold as one. Accounts a platform has already linked through a payment method, recovery address, or phone number stay linked.

If you need genuine anonymity or to evade a specific platform's enforcement, this is not the tool for that. The WebRTC check is diagnostic — it shows which IPs your session exposes, it does not hide them.
